## classifications.aops.fr
- URL: https://classifications.aops.fr
- Category: Classification & HR.
- What it is: platform dedicated to job classification frameworks, helping structure role references, clarify responsibilities and support HR transformation.
- Highlights: job weighting and role hierarchy modeling; support for revising classification grids; HR governance approach focused on pay equity.

## observatoire.aops.fr
- URL: https://observatoire.aops.fr
- Category: Data & Insights.
- What it is: health and benefits observatory designed to identify risk, monitor contributions and anticipate cost drift with actionable indicators.
- Highlights: secure intake of payroll, claims and coverage data; KPIs such as claims ratio, loss ratio and out-of-pocket trends; benchmarking, alerts and budget projections.

## outils.aops.fr
- URL: https://outils.aops.fr
- Category: Tools Suite.
- What it is: business toolkit for actuarial and employee-benefits use cases, built to speed up simulations, scenario comparisons and impact analysis.
- Highlights: expert calculators and simulation modules; scenario comparison and projection workflows; fast access to high-value business tools.

## tailortale.com
- URL used on the site: https://tailortale-eight.vercel.app
- Category: AI Product (AI storytelling).
- What it is: AI-assisted storytelling product for creating personalized stories with illustrations and audio narration through a playful, guided experience.
- Highlights: character and plot personalization; kid-focused illustrated story generation; smooth family-friendly experience.

## aops.fr
- URL: https://aops.fr
- Category: Corporate Website.
- What it is: primary website of AOPS Conseil, positioned around social protection, health benefits and retirement with an expert-led narrative.
- Highlights: clear positioning of the firm's core expertise; integrated consulting, training and innovation offerings; B2B-focused contact and conversion journey.

## actuarem.com
- URL: https://actuarem.com
- Category: SaaS Platform.
- What it is: digital actuarial platform that centralizes complex compensation calculations and supports faster decisions on social and regulatory topics.
- Highlights: automated regulatory calculations; built-in legal monitoring and rate references; time savings with lower error risk.

## threedaysin.com
- URL: https://threedaysin.com
- Category: Travel Planning.
- What it is: travel planning platform that turns a city break into a curated three-day itinerary, with places to visit and practical details.
- Highlights: curated three-day city itineraries; local points of interest organized into daily plans; practical information to support the trip.

## optendra.vercel.app
- URL: https://optendra.vercel.app
- Category: AI & Tender Response.
- What it is: AI platform using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and fine-tuned AI models to turn tender documents into clear, complete and compelling responses.
- Highlights: RAG grounded in tender files and source documents; fine-tuned models for response drafting; clear, complete tender responses.
